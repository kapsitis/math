=== SYSTEM ===
You convert scanned pages of a mathematics olympiad into Markdown.
Copy the problem texts exactly in their original language; do not
paraphrase, shorten or solve anything yourself unless explicitly asked.
Convert math formulas into LaTeX and enclose them in $...$ (inline) or
$$...$$ (display). Ignore page headers, the test instructions
("I osa. Lahendamisaega ...", "I часть. Время ..."), and the answer
placeholders (dotted lines "..........").
Start and terminate your whole output with three backquotes (```).

=== USER ===
The first 2 image(s) contain the short-answer test
("I osa") for grade 9, in Estonian. The remaining 3
image(s) are pages from the official solutions section
("Lahendused ja vastused"); use only the part titled
"9. klass, I osa" and ignore solutions of other grades or of "II osa".

Output every test problem of grade 9 in order, as follows
(N is the problem number as printed on the page):

```
# <lo-sample/> EE.PK.2006TEST.9.N

Problem text in Estonian (exactly as in the test pages).

<small>

* answer:ANSWER
* questionType:ShortAnswer

</small>

## Lahendus

Solution text in Estonian (exactly as in the solutions section).


# <lo-sample/> EE.PK.2006TEST.9.N+1
...
```

Rules:
- ANSWER is the final answer from the solutions section, written on one
  line in a short form (e.g. 165, 3/5, 2,4\pi cm, 3; 5). If the solutions
  section gives no explicit answer for a problem, derive it from the
  solution text; if even that is impossible, write "answer:TODO".
- The <small> section MUST contain both keys "answer" and
  "questionType:ShortAnswer" as shown.
- If the solution is only an answer without explanation, still add the
  "## Lahendus" section with that answer.
- Do not describe or redraw pictures. Where a problem contains a figure,
  insert a placeholder at the position of the figure:
  ![](EE.PK.2006TEST.9.N.png) for the first figure, then
  ![](EE.PK.2006TEST.9.NA.png), ![](EE.PK.2006TEST.9.NB.png), etc.
  for further figures of the same problem. Figures inside the solution
  get the same kind of placeholders, continuing the letter sequence.
- Separate problems by two empty lines.
